You can ask for access to your data, and for it to be corrected, deleted, restricted or transferred to you, and you can object to processing or withdraw consent at any time. Write to besasonos@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or to the data protection authority where you live.
